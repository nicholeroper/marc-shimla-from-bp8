Day-wise Itinerary
DAY
01

Delhi to Shimla
Arrival at Delhi Airport/Railway Station. Meet our
representative and proceed towards Shimla by private
cab. On arrival, check-in at the hotel and relax.

Meal: Dinner
Overnight Stay: Shimla

Day-wise Itinerary
DAY
02

Kufri & Local Shimla Sightseeing
After breakfast, proceed for a full-day excursion to Kufri, a
beautiful hill station located at an altitude of 2,622 meters.
En route visit Green Valley. Enjoy optional activities like Yak
Ride, Horse Ride, Mini Zoo, Fun World, Haunted House, etc.
In the evening, enjoy Local Shimla Sightseeing including:
Mall Road
Ridge
Lakkar Market
Scandal Point
Christ Church
Kali Bari Temple

Meal: Breakfast & Dinner
Overnight Stay: Shimla
(All adventure activities are optional & at own cost)

Day-wise Itinerary
DAY
03

Shimla to Kullu – Manali
(On the Way Sightseeing)
After breakfast, check-out and proceed towards Manali.
En-route sightseeing includes:
Sunder Nagar Lake
Pandoh Dam
Hanogi Mata Temple
Kullu Shopping Point
Kullu River Rafting Point
Arrival at Manali, check-in at hotel.

Meal: Breakfast & Dinner
Overnight Stay: Manali

Day-wise Itinerary
DAY
04

Manali Local Sightseeing
After breakfast, enjoy full-day Manali sightseeing:
Hadimba Devi Temple
Vashisht Temple & Hot Water Springs
Tibetan Monastery
Club House
Van Vihar
Return to hotel for dinner.

Meal: Breakfast & Dinner
Overnight Stay: Manali

DAY
05

Solang Valley – Atal Tunnel – Sissu

Meal: Breakfast & Dinner
Overnight Stay: Manali
